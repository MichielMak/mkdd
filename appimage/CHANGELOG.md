## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-11

### Features

- Dolphin 2609a AppImage for x86_64 and aarch64 that boots Mario Kart: Double Dash!! from your own disc image ([#1](https://github.com/MichielMak/mkdd/pull/1))
- `--dol` boots a `main.dol` built by the decomp with the disc's files ([#1](https://github.com/MichielMak/mkdd/pull/1))
- Gamepads are mapped to GameCube ports 1-4 automatically, and the mapping stays yours once you change it ([#2](https://github.com/MichielMak/mkdd/pull/2))
- Gamepads plugged in while Dolphin runs get the next free port ([#4](https://github.com/MichielMak/mkdd/pull/4))
- Settings window when started without arguments, and `--game` to start straight into the game in fullscreen for Steam and the Steam Deck ([#5](https://github.com/MichielMak/mkdd/pull/5))
